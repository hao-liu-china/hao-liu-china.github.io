I am a Zijiang Young Scholar, Principal Investigator, and Ph.D. Supervisor at the School of Geospatial Artificial Intelligence, East China Normal University (ECNU). My research interests lie in spatial intelligence, 3D vision, point cloud understanding, 3D object detection and tracking, scene understanding, and 3D reconstruction. The overarching goal of my research is to enable machines to perceive, understand, and reconstruct complex three-dimensional environments reliably.

Previously, I was a Postdoctoral Research Fellow at Nanyang Technological University, working with Prof. Ying He. I received my Ph.D. from Sun Yat-sen University in 2023, supervised by Prof. Yulan Guo, my M.E. from the National University of Defense Technology in 2018, and my B.E. from the University of Electronic Science and Technology of China in 2016.

OPENINGS

Ph.D. / Master / RA — I am always looking for self-motivated students and research interns to work on 3D vision and spatial intelligence, covering topics such as point cloud understanding, 3D detection and tracking, scene understanding, and reconstruction.
