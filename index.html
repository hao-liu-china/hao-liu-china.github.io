Hao Liu 刘浩

Zijiang Young Scholar · Principal Investigator · Ph.D. Supervisor

School of Geospatial Artificial Intelligence, East China Normal University

Shanghai, China · hliu@geoai.ecnu.edu.cn

I am a Zijiang Young Scholar, Principal Investigator, and Ph.D. Supervisor at the School of Geospatial Artificial Intelligence, East China Normal University (ECNU).

Before joining ECNU, I was a Postdoctoral Research Fellow at Nanyang Technological University, working with Prof. Ying He. I received my Ph.D. from Sun Yat-sen University in 2023, supervised by Prof. Yulan Guo, my M.E. from the National University of Defense Technology in 2018, and my B.E. from the University of Electronic Science and Technology of China in 2016.

Research interests: spatial intelligence, 3D vision, point cloud understanding, 3D object detection and tracking, scene understanding, and 3D reconstruction.

Openings. I am looking for self-motivated Ph.D./Master students and research interns interested in 3D vision and spatial intelligence. Please email me with your CV and transcripts.
